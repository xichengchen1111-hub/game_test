## Coplanar flicker repair (2026-10-12)

`ZFightRepair.Repair` runs before saving the ClockShop, ClockTower, StationB, RailTrain and HotelCar prefabs and before saving the Town (Town geometry), ClockShop and OtherTown scenes, with all scene roots checked together; saved generated prefab instances are fixed in scene passes. `CoplanarDetector` finds same-facing triangles within 0.6mm of the larger triangle's plane that overlap by at least 1cm2 (double-precision clipping around a local origin) and shade differently (material, UVs or light layers). The repair groups renderer+submesh+plane patches, colours overlapping patches largest-first with the smallest free step k x 2mm along the normal, steps duplicate triangles inside one patch by 1mm, and re-detects until clean (at most 6 passes). It saves render-mesh copies under `Assets/Generated/ZFightRepair/<label>/`, leaves colliders on the original meshes, and includes moving parts at their authored pose (the step is in their own frame). Unconverged or fixed-vs-fixed pairs fail the build. Fix coplanarity in generators first (police generator, hour-hand tails); never hide it with shader depth offsets or by disabling AO/shadows. After geometry changes run `ZFightScan.Run` and require 0 EXACT and 0 MOVING groups in `output/demo/zfight/scan.txt`.
